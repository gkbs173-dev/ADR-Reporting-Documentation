## 🔬 What is ADR Reporting?

**ADR Reporting** is the formal process of documenting and submitting information about adverse drug reactions to regulatory authorities, healthcare institutions, or pharmacovigilance databases.

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Spontaneous Reporting** | Voluntary reports by healthcare professionals or patients |
| **Mandatory Reporting** | Required by law for serious/unexpected ADRs |
| **Signal Detection** | Identifying new safety concerns from accumulated reports |
| **Periodic Safety Reports** | Regular summaries submitted by pharmaceutical companies |

---

## ⚠️ Why Reporting Matters

- 🛡️ **Patient Safety** — Helps prevent future harm to other patients
- 📊 **Signal Detection** — Identifies new or rare ADRs not found in clinical trials
- 🌍 **Global Pharmacovigilance** — Contributes to WHO's VigiBase (global ADR database)
- 📜 **Regulatory Action** — Can lead to label updates, warnings, or drug withdrawal
- 💊 **Safer Medicines** — Drives improvements in drug formulations and guidelines

## 🔄 The Reporting Process

```
Step 1: IDENTIFY          Step 2: DOCUMENT         Step 3: SUBMIT
─────────────────        ──────────────────        ───────────────
Recognize the ADR   →    Fill ADR Report Form  →   Submit to Authority
(Symptoms + Drug)        (All 5 Sections)          (CDSCO / WHO / etc.)

Step 4: ANALYZE
───────────────────
Causality Assessment
Signal Detection
Safety Action
```

### Reporting Timeline (Regulatory Requirements)

| ADR Severity | Reporting Deadline |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Fatal / Life-threatening** | Within **7 days** (expedited) |
| **Serious (non-fatal)** | Within **15 days** |
| **Non-serious** | Within **90 days** (periodic) |

## 📚 Key Learnings

- ✅ ADR reporting forms have **5 key sections**: Patient, Medicine, Reaction, Reporter, Additional Info
- ✅ **Seriousness** and **causality** are the two most critical fields in any ADR report
- ✅ India follows the **PvPI framework** under CDSCO for pharmacovigilance
- ✅ Timely reporting — especially for serious ADRs — is a **regulatory obligation**
- ✅ Every ADR report contributes to **global drug safety databases** like WHO VigiBase
- ✅ **Dechallenge** (stopping the drug) and **rechallenge** (restarting) are key concepts in causality
